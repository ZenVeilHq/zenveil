# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This is a professional community. We expect everyone to:

- Be respectful and constructive
- Focus on the work, not the person
- Accept feedback graciously
- Help others learn

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sharing actual vulnerabilities or exploits in public issues

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to [hello@zenveil.dev](mailto:hello@zenveil.dev). We will review all reports and take appropriate action, up to and including permanent bans from this organization's repositories.
